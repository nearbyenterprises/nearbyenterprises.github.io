Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Nearby Village! Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have about your data. By using the Nearby Village app, you agree to the practices described here.

Who We Are

Nearby Village is a community app that connects neighbours with local talents. It is operated from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, and this policy applies to the Nearby Village app and the nearbyvillage.ca website. Please also read our Terms & Conditions, which are available in the app.

Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following personal information:

  • Name (First and Last): To identify you within the service.
  • Email Address: For account creation, authentication, and essential communication.
  • Phone Number (Optional): As an optional contact method for other users should you create a talent profile. If you only browse, your phone number is never shown to other users.
  • Street Address: To calculate your location and show you nearby talents. If you only browse, your address is never shown to other users. If you create a talent listing, your street name is displayed on your listing, and your street number and unit number stay private unless you choose to share them.
  • Business Name (Optional): For users who operate as a talent.
  • Website and Social Links (Optional): Provided by talents to showcase their services and online presence.
  • Profile Photo (Optional): Talents can choose to upload a photo, which is shown on their talent profile. Other users can also add a photo to their account, but it is never shown to other users.
  • Ratings and Reviews (Optional): If you rate or review a talent, we collect your rating and comment. They are shown to other users with your first name, the first letter of your last name, and when the review was posted.
  • Usage and Technical Information: How you use the app, search terms, crash reports, and basic device details, as described below.

Nearby Village does not collect or process any payment or financial information, as all transactions occur offline between users and talents.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Display relevant talents and services within your preferred neighbourhood radius.
  • Provide access to talents' contact information for offline communication.
  • Send important service-related notifications.
  • Understand how the app is used, fix problems, and decide which services to add.
  • Review reports, enforce our Terms & Conditions, and prevent fraud, abuse, and safety problems.

Usage and Technical Information

We use Google Firebase to collect information about how the app is used and when it crashes, such as the screens and features you use (for example, viewing or contacting a listing), the search terms you type, and your device model, operating system version, app version, and approximate region. Crash reports also include a random account ID, but not your name, email address, or phone number.

We use this information to keep the app working, fix problems, and decide which services to add. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we keep it only as long as we need it for these purposes.

Information Visibility & Control

You have granular control over your information's visibility. By default, your personal contact information is private. You can choose to make parts of your information visible to other users, including:

  • Your contact email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your apartment or unit number.
  • Your street number.

Your first name, last name, and street name are the minimum information visible to other users in your radius if you are listed as a talent.

Please note that most communications and transactions between users and talents occur outside the Nearby Village app (such as by phone or email). You are responsible for the information you share during those interactions.

Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share limited data with essential third-party service providers as necessary to operate and maintain core app functionality — including, but not limited to, cloud hosting, authentication, analytics and crash reporting, mapping, and communication services (such as Google Firebase, Stadia Maps, and Brevo).

These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and data protection obligations to safeguard your information and may only process it on our behalf for legitimate operational purposes. For example, when you send us feedback or a report, the message (and any screenshot you attach) passes through our email delivery provider, Brevo, to our support mailbox. Your account and listing data is stored in Canada. Some of our providers process personal information outside Canada, where it may be subject to local laws.

We may also disclose information if required by law, such as in response to valid legal process, or when needed to protect someone's safety.

Location data is used only to provide proximity-based features within the app. Your address is converted to map coordinates using your phone's built-in address lookup service, and the neighbourhood map loads map tiles from Stadia Maps, which receives requests for the area shown on the map (not your name or account). We do not share location data with advertising providers.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information.
  • Update your information and modify your visibility settings at any time through your profile.
  • Delete your account, which will permanently remove your profile and personal information from our active systems (see Account Deletion below for what remains).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time by changing your notification settings, deleting your account, or contacting us.
  • You may also report any misuse, data concerns, or inappropriate behavior through the in-app Feedback & Reporting tool.

To ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information, to withdraw your consent, or to raise a concern about how we handle your information, email nearby.feedback@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Feedback and report messages you send us are kept for up to two years, then deleted. Usage and crash-report data is kept only as long as we need it for the purposes described above. Copies of deleted data may remain in our backups for a limited time until they are overwritten.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Nearby Village account at any time directly from within the app.

To delete your account:

  1. Open the Nearby Village app.
  2. Navigate to My Profile → Name & Password.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Once confirmed, your account and profile — including your name, email, phone number, address, profile photo, and the reviews other users have written about you — will be permanently deleted from our systems. This process is irreversible.

Reviews you have written about other users are not deleted when you delete your account. They remain visible with only your first name and the first letter of your last name. You can delete any of your reviews yourself before you delete your account. Usage and crash-report data collected by Google Firebase is not linked to your name, email address, or phone number, and is not removed when you delete your account.

Apart from the reviews and usage data described above, we do not retain your personal data after account deletion, except where required by law (for example, for fraud prevention or accounting compliance). Any such retained data is stored securely and automatically deleted after the applicable retention period.

If you experience any issues deleting your account, please contact us at nearby.feedback@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we will delete the account and its personal information, except where we must keep information for legal or safety reasons.

Our Child Safety Policy explains how we prevent and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation: read the Child Safety Policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the app and on our website with a new date, and tell you about significant changes through an in-app notice or by email. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
